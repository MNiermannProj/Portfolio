Autonomous UAV Development

Stevens Research Project

Contribution

Developed an AI-driven landing subsystem for autonomous UAVs targeting aerospace and defense applications. Leveraging Python and TensorFlow, I implemented computer vision and control algorithms that enhanced the drone’s stability during complex landing scenarios. My work optimized the weight-to-thrust ratio by 14%, achieved through aerodynamic refinements in SolidWorks and validation using ANSYS Fluent simulations, resulting in improved flight performance and efficiency.

More Detials
