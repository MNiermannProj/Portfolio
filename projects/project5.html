Nanotechnology Research

Research Project

Contributions

Applied MATLAB programming to extract intervals of intrinsic thermal conductance and conductivity as a function of laser radius to model the thermal transport properties of Fe-doped MoS₂, a promising two-dimensional material. The goal of this study was to better understand how nanoscale structural modifications influence heat transport in advanced materials for electronics and energy applications.

More Details

This work involved combining experimental data collection with computational modeling to analyze the thermal response at the nanoscale. I built MATLAB routines that incorporated uncertainty analysis, enabling accurate characterization of thermal parameters across varying conditions. Laser radius parameter values were gathered using Raman Spectroscopy. By linking material properties to thermal performance, this research provided insights into how doped 2D materials can be engineered for higher efficiency in next-generation devices.
