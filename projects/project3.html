VTOL UAV for Mid-Air Capture

Capstone Project

More About This Project

Led an eight-person engineering team in the design and development of a precision-capture VTOL UAV capable of intercepting and securing free-floating objects in midair, mimicking NASA’s proposed autonomous capture mission. I directed the system-level design, including propulsion layout and aerodynamic configuration in SolidWorks, and validated with FEA in ANSYS Mechanical to ensure structural robustness. Through iterative flight testing, we integrated onboard electronics, power distribution, and control hardware, successfully demonstrating stable hover and endurance performance.

More About This Project

My role included coordinating subsystem leads, validating thrust performance through static and dynamic testing, and refining capture mechanism feasibility within payload weight limits. This project required merging aerodynamics, controls, and systems integration into a cohesive UAV platform. The project pushed the boundaries of UAV design by addressing precision maneuvering in dynamic environments, demonstrating both technical feasibility and mission adaptability for aerospace applications.
